Status: **6a-1 designed and built (2026-11-28)**, with the Attendance page on the till (code and PIN, and for a manager who is in now). 6a-2, 6b and 6c are designed here only in outline. The overall plan is `docs/PLAN-MULTI-BRANCH.md` (P6) and the short summary is `docs/11-multi-branch.md` §5; this file is the detail.

## 1. Principles (from docs/11 §5, restated)

- **Salary data lives only at HQ.** A branch is sent an employee's code, name, active flag and attendance PIN hash. Never pay data.
- **Employee is not user.** A cleaner or a bagger has no sign-in. `employees` is HQ's own list; a link to a sign-in comes later (6c, self-service).
- **Punches are branch facts, append-only.** A mistake is fixed by an HQ adjustment with a reason and who made it, never an edit (hard rule 4).
- **The attendance PIN can only clock in and out.** It is a new credential, separate from a cashier's till PIN, so hard rule 20 is not weakened. It is rate-limited and locks out like a login, and no answer, log line or audit row ever carries it (rule 21).
- **Money and time are integers.** Time is whole minutes. (Pay comes in 6b.)

## 3. 6a-1 in detail

### 3.1 Who owns what

| Data | Owner | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| `employees` (code, name, active, PIN hash) | HQ | down, to **each branch the employee is assigned to** (a removed assignment sends a delete line to that branch) |
| `employee_branches` (who works where) | HQ | not sent (it decides who is sent where) |
| `attendance_punches` | the branch | up (the 33rd synced table, in the daily control) |
| `attendance_adjustments` | HQ | not sent (an HQ correction over the facts) |

### 3.2 At the branch

- **`POST /attendance/punch`** `{ code, pin }`, guarded like every route by a sign-in (any role: the person standing at the till or the manager's browser is signed in, and the employee clocks in beside them). It is **not** a new open door.
- The server decides **in or out** from the employee's last punch at THIS branch: none or an out is an in; an in is an out. A second punch within 60 seconds is refused (`PUNCHED_JUST_NOW`), so a double press is one punch.
- A wrong code and a wrong PIN give **one answer** (`INVALID_CREDENTIALS`); five failures for a code in ten minutes lock it for ten (`429 ATTENDANCE_LOCKED`). Failures are counted by the code as typed, so an unknown code and a known one look the same.
- The punch row holds the employee, in or out, the signed-in user who ran the screen, and the server's time. Audited as `attendance.punch` (no PIN).
- **`GET /attendance/today`** (manager): who is in now and who has gone, from this branch's punches today.
- A branch with no HQ link has no employees (they come from HQ), so the screen says so.

### 3.3 At HQ

- **Employees** (`/employees`, admin to change, any staff to read): create, edit (name, active), assign to branches (`PUT /employees/:id/branches`), set the PIN (`POST /employees/:id/pin`, 4 to 8 digits, hashed with Argon2id, never returned). Every change is in `hq_audit` through `staffTx`. A new employee's code is unique and never reused.
- **Adjustments** (`POST /attendance/adjustments`, admin): `add` a missing in or out at a time, or `cancel` a punch (by branch and punch id), each with a reason. Append-only; the daily view applies them.
- **`GET /attendance/days?from=&to=&employeeId=&branchId=`** (any staff): per employee and shop day, the punches (with the branch), worked minutes, and what is wrong. The pairing is: in the order of time, an in followed by an out is a span; **a span belongs to the shop day of its in** (a night shift is one day's work); two ins in a row (the earlier one), an out with no in, or an in still open at the end of the range are flagged (`double_in`, `out_without_in`, `open`) and add no minutes. Since the branch alternates in and out itself, these appear mainly when a punch is cancelled or an adjustment is added. Cancelled punches are ignored and added ones are included, marked.

### 3.4 What is not in 6a-1

Rosters and shift windows (6a-2), a punch's lateness, overtime and absence (6a-2), leave (6a-2), any pay (6b), an HQ screen (API only, like the rest of HQ so far), a camera or fingerprint (never planned), and a kiosk that needs no sign-in (it would be a new open door and needs its own decision).
