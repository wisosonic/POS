# 16 · HR: employees, attendance, payroll (P6)

Status: **6a-1 designed and built (2026-11-28)**, with the Attendance page on the till (code and PIN, and for a manager who is in now). 6a-2, 6b and 6c are designed here only in outline. The overall plan is `docs/PLAN-MULTI-BRANCH.md` (P6) and the short summary is `docs/11-multi-branch.md` §5; this file is the detail.

## 1. Principles (from docs/11 §5, restated)

- **Salary data lives only at HQ.** A branch is sent an employee's code, name, active flag and attendance PIN hash. Never pay data.
- **Employee is not user.** A cleaner or a bagger has no sign-in. `employees` is HQ's own list; a link to a sign-in comes later (6c, self-service).
- **Punches are branch facts, append-only.** A mistake is fixed by an HQ adjustment with a reason and who made it, never an edit (hard rule 4).
- **The attendance PIN can only clock in and out.** It is a new credential, separate from a cashier's till PIN, so hard rule 20 is not weakened. It is rate-limited and locks out like a login, and no answer, log line or audit row ever carries it (rule 21).
- **Money and time are integers.** Time is whole minutes. (Pay comes in 6b.)

## 3. 6a-1 in detail

### 3.1 Who owns what

| Data | Owner | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| `employees` (code, name, active, PIN hash) | HQ | down, to **each branch the employee is assigned to** (a removed assignment sends a delete line to that branch) |
| `employee_branches` (who works where) | HQ | not sent (it decides who is sent where) |
| `attendance_punches` | the branch | up (the 33rd synced table, in the daily control) |
| `attendance_adjustments` | HQ | not sent (an HQ correction over the facts) |

### 3.2 At the branch

- **`POST /attendance/punch`** `{ code, pin }`, guarded like every route by a sign-in (any role: the person standing at the till or the manager's browser is signed in, and the employee clocks in beside them). It is **not** a new open door.
- The server decides **in or out** from the employee's last punch at THIS branch: none or an out is an in; an in is an out. A second punch within 60 seconds is refused (`PUNCHED_JUST_NOW`), so a double press is one punch.
- A wrong code and a wrong PIN give **one answer** (`INVALID_CREDENTIALS`); five failures for a code in ten minutes lock it for ten (`429 ATTENDANCE_LOCKED`). Failures are counted by the code as typed, so an unknown code and a known one look the same.
- The punch row holds the employee, in or out, the signed-in user who ran the screen, and the server's time. Audited as `attendance.punch` (no PIN).
- **`GET /attendance/today`** (manager): who is in now and who has gone, from this branch's punches today.
- A branch with no HQ link has no employees (they come from HQ), so the screen says so.

### 3.3 At HQ

- **Employees** (`/employees`, admin to change, any staff to read): create, edit (name, active), assign to branches (`PUT /employees/:id/branches`), set the PIN (`POST /employees/:id/pin`, 4 to 8 digits, hashed with Argon2id, never returned). Every change is in `hq_audit` through `staffTx`. A new employee's code is unique and never reused.
- **Adjustments** (`POST /attendance/adjustments`, admin): `add` a missing in or out at a time, or `cancel` a punch (by branch and punch id), each with a reason. Append-only; the daily view applies them.
- **`GET /attendance/days?from=&to=&employeeId=&branchId=`** (any staff): per employee and shop day, the punches (with the branch), worked minutes, and what is wrong. The pairing is: in the order of time, an in followed by an out is a span; **a span belongs to the shop day of its in** (a night shift is one day's work); two ins in a row (the earlier one), an out with no in, or an in still open at the end of the range are flagged (`double_in`, `out_without_in`, `open`) and add no minutes. Since the branch alternates in and out itself, these appear mainly when a punch is cancelled or an adjustment is added. Cancelled punches are ignored and added ones are included, marked.

### 3.4 What is not in 6a-1

Rosters and shift windows (6a-2), a punch's lateness, overtime and absence (6a-2), leave (6a-2), any pay (6b), an HQ screen (API only, like the rest of HQ so far), a camera or fingerprint (never planned), and a kiosk that needs no sign-in (it would be a new open door and needs its own decision).

## 5. 6b: payroll (built 2026-11-28)

Salary data is the most sensitive in the system: it lives only at HQ (`cloud-hq/src/modules/payroll/`), every route is **admin only, reading included** (the HR and payroll roles come in 6c), and nothing here is ever sent to a branch. Money never goes into a log line, and the audit says who did what, never an amount.

### 5.1 The engine (`shared/src/payroll.ts`, a pure function)

`calculatePayslip({ profile, days, rules, rate, manual, loans })`: no clock, no database, no floats. Money is whole minor units (LBP 1, USD cent), time whole minutes, rates integer basis points. Products that can pass 2^53 are done in BigInt and rounded ONCE, half away from zero. Golden cases are in `branch-server/tests/unit/payroll.test.ts`.

- **Base pay:** *monthly* = the salary as it stands for the run; *daily* = the rate for each day with at least `dailyMinMinutes` worked; *hourly* = the rate for the regular minutes.
- **Overtime:** the minutes of each day past `overtimeAfterMinutes` (null: none), at the hourly value of the pay times the multiplier (`overtimeBp`, 15000 = 1.5). The hourly value is the hourly rate; a day's rate over `standardDayMinutes`; a month's salary over `workDaysPerMonth` days of that.
- **Rules are data** (`PayRule`): *percent* (a rate of the base, the base capped) or *brackets* (progressive: each rate on the part inside its bracket); on the *gross* (social security, which may *lower the taxable amount*) or on the *taxable* amount (income tax); the *employee* side is taken from the pay, the *employer* side is a cost beside it. A rule has its own currency; a person paid in the other currency is converted at the run's rate and back. A profile lists the rule codes that apply to that person.
- **Manual lines** (a bonus, an absence deduction) are added as given; **loan installments** come last and are never more than what is owed nor than the pay that is left. Always `net = gross - deductions`.
- **Absence is not deducted automatically** (it needs the roster, 6a-2): HR enters it as a manual deduction. A monthly salary is paid whole.

### 5.2 What is stored, and how it stays honest

- `pay_profiles`, `payroll_rules` (+ `payroll_rule_brackets`), `loans`, `loan_repayments` are **dated or appended, never edited** (triggers). The profile and rule in force for a period are the latest that had begun by the period's last day, so an old period is always worked out with what was true then. **No rate is built in:** the accountant adds the rules, and the first run is to be checked against their figures.
- A **run** (`payroll_runs`) is for a period of shop days and goes `draft -> calculated -> approved -> paid -> locked`, only forward (a trigger). It can be calculated again, with changed manual lines, until it is approved. **Once approved, its payslips, their lines and its manual inputs are frozen by triggers** and its period is fixed; a locked run does not change at all and no run is ever deleted. A correction is a manual line in the NEXT run. Two approved runs may not cover the same day (`PERIOD_ALREADY_PAID`).
- Each **payslip** keeps the profile used, the version (id and start day) of every rule used, the exchange rate of the run, and the employee's code and name as they were. Its lines are `earning`, `deduction` and `employer`, in order.
- **Paying** turns each loan installment on the slips into a repayment, once (a second pay is refused): what is owed goes down exactly as much as was taken.

### 5.3 The gate

Payroll is worked out from attendance, so `calculate` refuses (`409 BRANCH_BEHIND`, naming the branch) while a connected branch with an employee in the run has not **proved the period's last day**: the daily control (docs/12 §24) must hold an `ok` comparison for that day. The last day must also be finished (`PERIOD_NOT_FINISHED`). Warnings that do not block are listed on the run: an employee with attendance flags (open, doubled), a loan in another currency (not deducted), and a negative net; employees with no pay profile are skipped and listed.

### 5.4 Not in 6b

Roster-based absence, lateness and leave (6a-2); payslip view, print and self-service, the HR roles and the audit of payslip views (6c); an HQ screen; advances given from a till; bank files; the statutory rates themselves.

## 6. 6a-2: shifts, rosters, holidays and leave (built 2026-11-28)

All of it is HQ's and derived or planned, never a fact: `cloud-hq/src/modules/hr/schedule.ts`. Staff read (any role); an admin changes (the HR roles are 6c).

- **Shifts** (`shift_templates`): a name, a start (minutes from the shop's midnight), a length (a night shift runs past midnight) and a **grace** (default 5 minutes: later or earlier than that is late or early). A shift in use is retired, never deleted.
- **Roster** (`rosters`): one shift per employee per day at one branch, set or cleared in bulk (`PUT /roster`, a null shift clears a day). The employee must be active and assigned to the branch and the shift must be active. A roster is a plan, so it can be changed; every change is audited. It is **not sent to the branches** (a branch has no use for it yet; the attendance screen shows who is in, not who should be).
- **Holidays** (`holidays`) and **leave**: types with a paid or unpaid flag; a request is `pending` then `approved` or `rejected`, and an approved leave can be `cancelled`. A decision is final (a trigger), the dates never change, a leave is never deleted, and two approved leaves of one person may not overlap. Requests are entered by an admin on the person's behalf until self-service (6c).
- **The plan beside what happened** (`GET /attendance/schedule`, at most 62 days): for each rostered day, `worked` (with `lateMinutes` when the first clock-in was later than the shift start plus its grace, `earlyLeaveMinutes` likewise at the end, and `overMinutes` past the scheduled length), `absent` (no work, no leave, no holiday, and the day is over), `on_leave` (with the type and whether it is paid), `holiday`, or `scheduled` (a day not yet over). Attendance on a day with no roster, for a person who has a roster, is `unscheduled`. A night shift's out is on the day it began. The shift's start is the shop's local time (a clock change at midnight is off by an hour on two days a year at most).
- **Payroll** gets a warning, not a change: `calculate` lists `ABSENCE` for each employee with absent days or unpaid leave on the roster, so HR adds a deduction as a manual line. Pay is not changed automatically (the daily wage already pays only worked days; a monthly salary is paid whole until HR decides otherwise).
- **Not built:** a roster pushed to the branch, a punch flagged at the till for being outside its shift, leave balances and accrual, public holidays by rule, a roster screen (API only), and rostering from a template for a week.
